Creamy Garlic Parmesan Pasta

Chef John
Content Creator
YouTube
Servings
4
Prep
5 min
Cook
15 min
Total
20 min
High confidence · 92/100

Ingredients

6 items
Spaghetti 400g
Garlic 4 cloves
Heavy cream 200ml
Parmesan 100g grated
Butter 2 tbsp
Salt 1 tsp

Steps

5 steps
1

Boil the pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add spaghetti and cook according to package directions until al dente, usually 8-10 minutes.

2

Sauté the garlic

While pasta cooks, melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant and golden.

3

Add cream

Pour in the heavy cream and stir well. Let simmer for 3-4 minutes until the sauce starts to thicken slightly.

4

Combine pasta and sauce

Drain the pasta, reserving 1/2 cup of pasta water. Add pasta to the skillet and toss with the creamy garlic sauce.

5

Add parmesan and serve

Sprinkle grated parmesan over the pasta and toss until melted. Add pasta water if needed for consistency. Season with salt and serve immediately.
